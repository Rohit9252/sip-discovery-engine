STP lets a user set up automatic transfers from one mutual fund to another, making it easier for them to manage their investments without manual effort. Suppose you’ve invested in a liquid fund but want to gradually move those funds into other investments over time. With STP, you can set up instructions to move money from one fund to another automatically, saving you time and hassle. Typically, STP allows transfers only within funds managed by the same AMC.
