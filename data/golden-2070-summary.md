## Four patterns behind most misses

1. **First-time is over-counted.** Of the 20 posts the engine called first-time, 4 started 2 or more years earlier (#1, #3, #6, #16) and 3 do not say (#8, #25, #26). Only 13 of 20 hold up. Every headline number uses first-time posts.
2. **"Help me choose funds" (T10) swallows beginner questions.** "Where do I start", "will I get advice" and "is the NAV a problem" went to T10 instead of T5 (#10, #11, #23). The code rule that sends "suggest" or "advice" to T10 is the likely cause. T10 is 581 of 968 first-time needs, so T5 is probably undercounted.
3. **The evidence rule wipes implied answers.** Planning and just-started posts lose their stage and outcome because their wording ("planning to", "want to start", "just started") is not in the code's list of time words. Before starting and setting up, the stages where first-timers are, are undercounted.
4. **Asking on Reddit or YouTube is rarely recorded** as going outside the app (13 of 15 misses), although the instructions say it counts.
